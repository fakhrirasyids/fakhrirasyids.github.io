02 / About

Engineering with the whole system in mind.

I'm a Tech Lead and Mobile Engineer with 4+ years of experience building Android, iOS, desktop, web, SDK, and hardware-connected clinical systems across healthcare, government, industrial, education, retail, and enterprise domains. My recent work focuses on Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, Clean Architecture, USB/BLE medical device integrations, offline-first workflows, and production systems used in hospital environments across Southeast Asia.

I have led 5+ engineers, shipped 5+ medical applications, supported 10+ USB/BLE medical devices, and partnered closely with product, design, firmware, backend, and client teams to turn complex clinical requirements into reliable release-ready software. I also hold a Bachelor's degree in Informatics Engineering from Universitas Dian Nuswantoro with a 3.83 / 4.00 GPA, and I keep a strong engineering bias toward maintainable architecture, testing, CI/CD, mentoring, and practical product delivery.
