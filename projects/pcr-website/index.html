PCR Website

01 / Overview

Product landing page for the PCR device built with Vue, Tailwind CSS, and vue-i18n supporting English, Indonesian, and Chinese. The page presents the device's nucleic acid amplification workflow, rapid on-site diagnostic capabilities, and clinical use cases with an animated hero, feature highlights, and seamless language switching across all three locales.
