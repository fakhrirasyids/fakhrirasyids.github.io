Stethoscope

01 / Overview

The Stethoscope app is a Kotlin Multiplatform application built with Jetpack Compose, running natively on Android and iOS from a single shared codebase. It pairs with BLE-enabled stethoscopes to stream high-fidelity heart and lung sounds and visualize them in real time. Devices that support a USB connection can also be wired in directly for more stable, latency-free audio streaming. Recordings are attached to patient encounters for longitudinal tracking, and the offline-first data layer keeps sessions intact even without a network connection.
